## Playback and usage

Use official player controls. Do not hide the player, title or branding, strip the audio, suppress ads, or award rewards for viewing. Rewards depend on correct quiz answers only and listening is optional. Ads, sign-in restrictions, publisher changes and regional blocks can affect a source. Each round provides an original video link and a text clue.

YouTube references: [player parameters](https://developers.google.com/youtube/player_parameters), [developer policies](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies). A future public audio-only quiz needs permitted excerpt files and documented scope (web quiz, edits, territory, duration and attribution). Do not infer those rights from oEmbed availability.

# Development roadmap

Updated 2026-11-11.

## Implemented

- Three solo modes, five-round games, Normal 20s and Quick 10s answering.
- Cinema review catalog: 18 questions across 12 Telugu films (2000–2026) with official publisher sources.
- Account-linked coin wallet, welcome bonus, seven-day calendar, daily claim streak, three daily missions, transaction activity, XP levels and four trophies.
- Server-selected/validated coin games with once-only entry/reward processing and 24-hour pause/resume.
- Empty-wallet choices and unrestricted free practice without wallet rewards.
- Clearly labelled local test checkout (100/300/750 coins); no actual payment service.
- Review guide, source previews/checks, automated economy/profile checks and dated handoff documentation.
- Username/password registration and login, existing-session conversion, avatar/display-name editing and a profile statistics dashboard.

## Next stages

1. Receive the user's permitted Music/Songs/Dialogues, organize them by category and attach verified answers for randomized playback.
2. Evaluate economy numbers and difficulty with the client; add difficulty/era filters and a larger balanced catalog.
3. Add a shared daily challenge and spoiler-free result sharing.
4. Add weekly missions, cosmetic progression, comeback rewards and friend challenges after the core economy review.
5. Before real purchasing: choose a provider, review fees/requirements, implement verified provider events, orders, retry handling, payment failure/refund flows, account recovery and operational controls. Keep test credits separate from any future paid balance.
6. Prepare public hosting and content permissions when review scope is accepted. Real audio licensing and visible-video spoiler limitations remain unresolved for a blind public game.

No real payment, external messages or public deployment occurred in this revision. A free implementation can simulate checkout; real provider fees must not be described as zero without verification.
